# PHASE 1: MULTIMODE INTERFERENCE (MMI), SLOTTED & MZI DESIGNS

## Overview
Phase 1 investigated alternative photonic architectures for non-volatile spatial routing, focusing on:
1. 1x2 Multimode Interference (MMI) splitters with phase-change patches.
2. Slotted silicon and silicon-nitride (SiN) embedded waveguides.
3. Compact Mach-Zehnder Interferometers (MZIs) and Total Internal Reflection (TIR) switches.

## Key Outcomes & Why Abandoned
* **MMI Footprint Penalty:** Compact 1x2 MMI cavities required wide multi-mode regions (w > 1.5 um, length > 8-15 um), violating the sub-6 um^2 active footprint limit.
* **Slot Waveguide Scattering:** Slotted waveguides induced significant boundary scattering (> 1.2 dB loss) at the transition between strip and slot modes.
* **MZI Complexity:** Required dual 3-dB splitters (50:50 power division), violating the strict pure spatial routing constraint (strictly NO 3-dB couplers).
